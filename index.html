Godwake

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

Godwake is a Christian morning alarm. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

Who we are

Godwake is provided by the developer of the Godwake app. For questions, use the public Support page. You do not need the app installed.

Data stored on your device

Godwake is local-first. The following stays on your phone and is not uploaded to our servers (we do not operate an account or cloud sync backend in the current version):

  • Alarm times, labels, sound and vibrate preferences, and any 30-second wake clips you import (kept only on this phone)
  • App settings (language, appearance, snooze length, alarm audio mode)
  • Streak / completion dates
  • Optional morning gratitude notes you type

Uninstalling the app deletes this data. You can also wipe it in Settings → Delete all data without uninstalling. Godwake does not operate its own cloud backup. On iOS, Apple’s iCloud device backup may still include app data (journal and settings) unless you exclude Godwake from iCloud Backup in system Settings.

Permissions

Notifications / alarms

Required so Godwake can wake you at the time you set. On Android this may include exact alarms and full-screen alarm presentation. On iOS this uses Apple AlarmKit authorization.

Microphone

Godwake does not use the microphone. Morning challenges are tap-based. The optional Android “silent window” (alarm sound paused while you answer) ends on a timer and uses gentle vibration only — no speech recognition and no audio capture.

Battery / background (Android)

Some phones restrict background apps. Godwake may ask you to allow unrestricted battery use so the alarm is not killed overnight. That setting is controlled by your device manufacturer.

Crash reports

This version does not send crash reports. Nothing is uploaded when the app closes unexpectedly.

No sale of data

We do not sell your personal data. The current version does not include advertising SDKs. There is no advertising or marketing analytics.

Children

Godwake is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the journal to store sensitive data about minors.

Scripture text

Bible translations shown in the app are attributed in Settings → Verse text. Licensing details live with those notices.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app gains networking, accounts, or payments. The “Last updated” date will change.

Contact

Use the public Support page. You do not need the app installed.
