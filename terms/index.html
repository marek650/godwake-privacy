Godwake

Terms of Use

Last updated: 14 August 2026

Godwake is a local morning alarm. It runs on your phone. There is no account and no sign-in.

You set an alarm. When it rings, you complete a short morning — a verse, or a blessing when scripture for that language is not included. You may optionally type a morning gratitude note. That note stays on this phone.

This version does not sell subscriptions or in-app purchases.

The alarm cannot stop you from turning the phone off. Exact timing depends on the system permissions you grant.

For help, use the public Support page. You do not need the app installed.
